#  E-Cell UIET Kurukshetra — Official Landing Page

Hey everyone!  
This repository contains the official landing page for **E-Cell UIET Kurukshetra**, built to give our college startup culture a proper web presence. 

The goal was to build something clean, fast, and modern that showcases our upcoming events, team leadership, and vision for student founders at UIET.

 **Live Site:** [shivanshgh.github.io/E-cell-Landing-page](https://shivanshgh.github.io/E-cell-Landing-page/)

---

##  What's Inside

- **Hero Section:** Clean intro with dark-mode aesthetic and animated background canvas.
- **About Section (`01`):** Overview of E-Cell UIET KUK, vision, and core pillars (Mentorship, Incubation, Workshops).
- **Events & Flagships (`02`):** Display cards for upcoming events like *E-Summit PitchFest*, *AI & Product Management Workshop*, and *Founder's Unplugged*.
- **Leadership (`03`):** Team showcase introducing the Overall Coordinator, Operations Lead, and Corporate Relations Lead.
- **Membership Callout (`04`):** Direct CTA section for students who want to join the cell.
- **Contact & Footer (`05`):** Campus location, official email (`ecell@uietkuk.ac.in`), and social connections.
