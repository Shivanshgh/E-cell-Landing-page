01 · Who We Are

Fostering Innovation at UIET

The Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell) of University Institute of Engineering & Technology (UIET), Kurukshetra University, is a student-driven organization dedicated to nurturing the spirit of entrepreneurship among students.

We provide a platform for aspiring founders to turn innovative ideas into successful ventures through interactive workshops, mentorship programs, hackathons, and guest lectures from industry veterans.

Institute
UIET, Kurukshetra University
Vision
Building an ecosystem for student founders & innovators
Focus
Startups, Incubation, Tech Innovation, Skill Building
Location
Kurukshetra, Haryana, India

Mentorship & Guidance

Direct access to alumni, startup founders, and industry leaders who help refine business models.

Incubation Support

Assistance with prototype building, pitch deck creation, and connect with seed funding networks.

Workshops & Bootcamps

Practical sessions covering Business Analytics, Product Design, AI tools, and Pitching Skills.
